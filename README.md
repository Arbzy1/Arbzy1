## Aden Butt

Computer Science graduate (BSc, 2:1, Cardiff University), currently finishing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence. Based in Epsom, Surrey. Looking for a first full-time role in IT support or data.

Two things sit behind most of what I build.

**IT and systems.** My first technical experience was a support placement in a school IT department. Since then I have run a Windows Server home lab covering Active Directory, Group Policy, DNS and DHCP, plus networking work where I measure whether a change actually helped rather than assuming it did. One travel-router project took throughput from around 3 Mbps to a steady 30-50 Mbps down and 130 Mbps up, by testing settings one at a time.

**Data and AI.** My MSc dissertation is a computer vision pipeline over 16,992 Google Street View images across four UK cities. It combines seven models (YOLOv8, YOLO-World, SegFormer, CLIP, DINOv2, Depth Anything V2) to extract street-level features such as greenery, building density and vehicle counts, then tests them against the Welsh Index of Multiple Deprivation, Land Registry prices, recorded crime and NO2 pollution. Trained on three cities, it predicted deprivation in an unseen fourth city at a moderate Spearman correlation of 0.48.
